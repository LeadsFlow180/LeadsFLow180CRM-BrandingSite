# LeadsFlow180 Team Profiles: Website Content Pack

**Purpose:** Site-ready copy for 21 AI teammate profile pages, following the Lee Park reference layout. This pack includes page copy, portfolio concepts, core skills, FAQs, a three-step engagement flow, and a useful guide section for each person.

## Publishing notes

- Keep each page about the work the specialist can help a small business do. The team members are AI teammates; label them clearly as AI on the site and do not imply human credentials, client results, or completed case studies that do not exist.
- Portfolio concepts below are **sample demonstrations**. Label mockups “Sample concept” until replaced with real, permissioned work. Do not show invented performance results.
- Use the FAQ answers as visible page content and FAQPage structured data only when the same Q&A is visible to visitors. Structured data can help machines understand content; it does not guarantee rich results or rankings.
- The guide content is designed to live on the page as useful, crawlable content. The “Download” link can point to a designed PDF version when ready. Add a descriptive title, short summary, author/reviewer, last-updated date, and internal links.
- Replace bracketed items such as `[Chat with ...]` and `[Download PDF]` with working links. Do not publish placeholder links.
- Short, direct answers support answer engines. Keep claims current, source-check changing advice, and avoid repeating keyword phrases unnaturally.

## Shared page structure

Breadcrumbs: Home → Our AI Office → Meet the Team → [Name]

Hero: `[SPECIALTY]` / `[NAME]` / `[AI ROLE]` / intro / `[Ask NAME about your business]` / “Free 5-minute chat. No obligation.”

Then: Facts About [Name] → Portfolio → About [Name] + Core Skills → FAQs → How to Get Started → Free Quick Guide.

## Cross-page SEO/AEO implementation checklist

- Give each page a unique title and description; use the specialist’s real service topic and the business audience naturally.
- Use one descriptive H1 per page. Organize sections with clear H2s such as “About,” “Core Skills,” “Frequently Asked Questions,” and the guide title.
- Use consistent canonical URLs and link profiles from the team directory. Link each guide to relevant service pages and other team profiles where helpful.
- Add a visible author line such as “LeadsFlow180 AI Team” and a human reviewer/editor line if a person verifies the advice. Show publication and review dates for guidance that may change.
- Add Person/Organization structured data only for accurate identity details. Use FAQPage markup only for visible FAQs and only where it complies with current search-engine policies; do not expect special search display.
- For downloadable PDFs, include the guide text on the page too, use accessible headings and links, add descriptive filenames, and make the PDF readable by assistive technology.
- Use real portfolio work only with permission and accurate context. For mockups, mark them as sample concepts and avoid fabricated client names, statistics, testimonials, credentials, or results.
- Optimize images with descriptive alt text, sensible file sizes, and responsive dimensions. Do not put essential FAQ or guide text only inside images or PDFs.
- Review search guidance and platform policies periodically. Search behavior and eligibility rules change; no AEO, SEO, or GEO tactic guarantees placement.
